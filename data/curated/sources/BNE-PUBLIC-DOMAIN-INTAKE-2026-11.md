# BNE Digital — public-domain works intake

- Official catalogue: https://datos.gob.es/es/catalogo/ea0019768-biblioteca-digital-documentos-en-dominio-publico1
- Publisher: Biblioteca Nacional de España.
- Dataset: digitised public-domain works; metadata includes digital-object and OCR-text links where available.
- Declared reuse licence: CC BY 4.0.
- Current distribution: UTF-8 CSV, JSON, TXT and XML; last published update 2024-12-04.
- Priority: Spanish philosophy, mathematics, science, history and reference works, selected using bibliographic subjects and title/author metadata.
- Admission rule: keep only actual OCR text files with source identifier, title, author, canonical BNE link, source revision/date and per-item public-domain/CC BY attribution. Metadata-only rows are not training documents.
- This source is a strong official Spanish-library candidate; no full-text document count is claimed until the OCR links and files have been audited.
